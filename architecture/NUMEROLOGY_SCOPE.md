# Professional Numerology Scope

Professional Numerology is a future independent knowledge domain.

Numerology applies only to an individual property in relation to the intended user or users. Relevant inputs may include the apartment or house number, property name where applicable, and the intended occupant's date of birth or name when required by the selected numerology method.

Numerology must not independently score or assess an entire tower, a complete building, a residential project, or a builder portfolio. Tower and building reports remain Vastu and project-aggregation outputs.

The platform must preserve separate Professional Vastu and Professional Numerology assessments. Their scores must not be directly compared or averaged. Any Overall Compatibility result is a separate alignment assessment and must not alter either underlying result.
