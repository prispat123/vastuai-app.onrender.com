# Knowledge Engine Expansion Policy

Both Vastu and Numerology Knowledge Engines are living, versioned expert systems.

## Vastu

Continue adding structured Knowledge Objects for:
- rooms and directions;
- plot and building conditions;
- water, services and circulation;
- apartment, independent-house and commercial contexts;
- exceptions, limitations and remedies.

## Numerology

Continue adding structured Knowledge Objects for individual properties and
their intended users only.

Future Numerology areas may include:
- expanded number relationships;
- compound property numbers;
- name-number methods as separate profiles;
- multiple intended occupants;
- method-specific recommendations and exceptions.

## Boundaries

Numerology does not independently assess towers, buildings or projects.

Vastu and Numerology scores remain independent. They must not be averaged or
directly compared. A future Overall Compatibility assessment will be a
separate, transparent engine.
